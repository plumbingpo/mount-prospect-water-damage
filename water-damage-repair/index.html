Drying a home is only half the job. Even with normal moisture readings, you are left with opened walls, flooring that is gone and exposed subfloor. Rebuilding all of that is what water damage repair means. For Mount Prospect homeowners, our crew installs replacements for anything unsalvageable and blends them with the existing finishes until the space stops looking like a job site.

Repair work covers a wide range. A minor loss can need just a couple of feet of wallboard plus baseboard and a coat of paint. Large jobs can include insulation, whole drywall sheets, subfloor, laminate or hardwood, cabinet boxes, doors and trim. We replace materials like for like where possible, and we tell you plainly when an exact match is not available so you can choose.

Dry first, then cover: that order never changes. A moisture meter has to show normal levels in the subfloor and framing before new material is installed. Cover wood that is still damp, and mold grows unseen behind the new paint. We also give you a written scope and a schedule before work starts, so you know what is being rebuilt and in what order.

Warning Signs for Water Damage Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Walking over the formerly wet area, you feel soft spots in the floor.
  • After drying, the hardwood is still cupped, crowned or lifting.
  • Trim around doors and along the floor is bowed and will not lie flat.
  • Wall cavities remain open where drywall was removed for drying.
  • Paint is bubbling, peeling or stained brown where water ran down the wall.
  • Tiles sound hollow, have come loose or show cracked grout since water got beneath them.
  • Seams are opening, or edges are curling and swelling, on vinyl plank or laminate.

The Benefits of Professional Water Damage Repair

Repair after water damage is not ordinary remodeling. A rebuilder must know the path the water took, the harm it caused and if the structure hidden by the wall is actually dry. Water Damage Repair, Step by Step

We follow a proven process to ensure every job is completed to the highest standards. Here's how we handle water damage repair from start to finish:

1

Verify Dryness First

Our crew meters the remaining drywall, the subfloor and the framing one last time. Until the readings are normal for every material, no reconstruction happens, and no moisture gets trapped.

2

Write the Scope

You receive a room by room list of what will be replaced, the materials we plan to use and the expected schedule. If a claim is open, we line the scope up with the adjuster.

3

Reconstruct and Install

Open wall cavities get new insulation, the subfloor is fixed, and drywall goes up, then is taped and finished. Flooring, cabinets and trim are then installed in the order that protects the finished surfaces.

4

Final Finishes and Walkthrough

The last stage covers texture matching, primer, paint and putting hardware, doors and baseboards back. Together we then walk through each room, and every listed item is corrected before the job is closed.

Maintenance Tips for Water Damage Repair

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Caulk around tubs, showers and sinks yearly so water stays out of the wall.
  • No wall or floor should be sealed by any contractor until you have seen final moisture readings.
  • Use moisture resistant drywall and backer board when rebuilding bathrooms, kitchens and laundry rooms.
  • Fix the source first, and only then patch or paint a water stain.
  • Rebuilt bathroom walls last longer when the exhaust fan runs through and after each shower.
  • File the written scope and photos of the completed repair with your household papers.
