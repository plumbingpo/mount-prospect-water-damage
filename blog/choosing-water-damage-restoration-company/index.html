How to Find the Right Restoration Company in Mount Prospect, IL

Home / Blog / Choosing a Water Damage Restoration Company in Mount Prospect, IL

When water damage strikes your Mount Prospect, IL property, choosing the right restoration company is one of the most important decisions you'll make. Your out-of-pocket cost, the pace of the work, the thoroughness of the repair, and the odds of secondary trouble such as mold months later all depend on who you hire. Sadly, some operators in this industry are not qualified, and they can leave behind unfinished work, padded bills, or worse. Here is what to look for when sorting the best restoration firms from everyone else.

Confirm the Crew Is IICRC Certified

In the restoration industry, the leading credential comes from the Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification (IICRC). To earn certification, a technician trains rigorously in restoration science, covering mold prevention, moisture measurement, structural drying, and proper extraction technique. Look for two current IICRC certifications on the crew: WRT (Water Damage Restoration) and ASD (Applied Structural Drying). Steer clear of any firm unable to show proof of them.

Ask for Proof of Insurance and Licenses

Every restoration company working on your property should carry comprehensive insurance including general liability and workers' compensation. Request certificates of insurance and verify they're current. A company lacking coverage can leave you on the hook when someone gets hurt or more property is damaged mid-project. Check as well that it has every business license Mount Prospect or Illinois rules call for.

Ask How Fast They Respond

Water damage is a time-sensitive emergency. Costs and damage climb with every hour that passes. The restoration company you choose should offer true 24/7 emergency response with fast arrival times in your area. Get a specific answer on how long they take to reach Mount Prospect, and make sure a live person picks up the emergency line instead of voicemail or an answering service that slows things down.

Find Out What Equipment and Methods They Use

Professional restoration requires professional equipment. Three questions cover it: do they use thermal imaging to detect moisture, are their dehumidifiers LGR units (the industry standard), and is extraction done with truck-mounted equipment, which outperforms portable-only operations? Ask how drying is monitored too, because a reputable company checks moisture every day and repositions equipment according to the readings instead of setting it up and walking away.

Read Reviews and Call References

You can learn a lot about past performance from what customers post online. Search Yelp, Google, and the BBB to see if customer satisfaction, professional conduct, and work quality hold up consistently. The most useful reviews talk about communication, punctuality, help with insurance, and how the finished restoration turned out. It is also fair to ask for the names of recent Mount Prospect water damage customers you can contact.

Know How They Price and Bill Insurance

Expect a detailed estimate up front, along with a plain explanation of the pricing, from any firm worth hiring. Good firms often price jobs with industry-standard software such as Xactimate, which insurers know and accept. Ask whether the company works directly with insurance carriers and whether they can bill your insurance directly. Be wary of any company that wants a large payment up front or will not work with insurance.
